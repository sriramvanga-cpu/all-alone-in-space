# Playing Nova Grid

A guide for players, and for anyone explaining the game at the booth. All the numbers here are the game's real ones.

## The story

Vulcan's core collapsed, and the planet broke apart. You captain the last starship, and the blast damaged its life support: there are 15 minutes of air left. Find a world you can live on, and land there before the air runs out.

## The goal

**Land on a habitable world before the 15-minute clock runs out.** You win when a landing settles, which is when its readings come back habitable. Your score is:

> **Points = fuel left + 1 point for every 10 seconds left**

So a pilot who lands with 10 fuel and 4:10 on the clock scores 10 + 25 = **35 points**. The clock stops the moment you press Land, so the landing film and the readings that follow cost no points.

You lose if the clock runs out, or if you're **stranded**: you can't afford any lane out, and there's no planet left to try where you are.

## Before you fly

1. **Registration.** Enter your name and phone number. The phone number is only used to contact prize winners, and it never appears on the board.
2. **The intro film** tells the story in about 28 seconds. **Skip** (or Escape) jumps past it.
3. **How to play** shows the game in six short scenes: goal, fly, hop, refuel, answer, land. Step through with the chips or the arrow keys, then press **Start the clock**. **Skip** starts the clock too.

## The top bar

| Meter | What it means |
|---|---|
| Fuel | Pays for flying lanes between solar systems. You start with 10. |
| Power | Pays for scans and for hops between planets. You start with 10. |
| Credits | Earned at fuel stops and from right answers. Spent in the shop. |
| Clock | Time left. It starts at 15:00 and turns urgent under 2 minutes: the screen edges glow red and frost over, and pulse faster in the last 30 seconds. |
| Points if you land | What you'd score if a landing settled right now. |

## The star map

You start at the **station** in the middle of the star map. Solar systems sit around it in three rings, joined by **lanes**.

- **Scan** (1 power) shows the systems one lane away from where you are.
- **Tap a system** to fly the lane to it. The pill on each lane shows its fuel cost. At the starting price, a lane from the station costs 5 fuel, and a lane between systems costs 8, 12 or 16, depending on whether it reaches the inner, middle or outer ring.
- **Enter** a system to see its planets.
- **Drag** to look around the map, and **scroll** (or pinch on a trackpad) to zoom.

Each planet is marked on the star map: a **drop** for a fuel stop, and a **!** for a possible home.

### Flight events

About one lane flight in three (never your first) runs into something, and you pick one of two options:

| Event | Option 1 | Option 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Debris field | Swerve around: −1 fuel | Push through slowly: −20 s |
| Ion storm | Raise shields: −5 credits | Wait it out: −30 s |
| Drifting tanker | Siphon it: +2 fuel, −30 s | Fly on |
| Distress call | Tow it: +10 credits, −30 s | Fly on |
| Gravity slingshot | Slingshot: −1 fuel, +30 s | Fly on |

## Inside a solar system

Planets circle the sun. **Tap a planet to hop there.** A hop costs power by how far apart the two planets are *right now*, so a hop is cheaper when they line up. Each planet's pill shows the current price.

Power recharges while you're in a system: 1 every 20 seconds, up to 10. To leave, use the **Star map** button under the jump point.

The sun's type (yellow star, red giant, black hole, and so on) is only for looks; every system plays the same. So are the extras: some systems have an asteroid belt between two orbits, some planets have a moon or two, and a comet crosses now and then.

## Fuel stops (planets with a drop)

Landing at a fuel stop starts a **fuel minute**: one minute of a quick game that earns credits. There are eight games, and each pilot gets them in their own shuffled order.

| Game | What you do | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure valve | Tap Vent while the needle on the round gauge is in the green zone | 1 for every 2 |
| Reactor tap | Four coloured cells light up one by one, each with its own note. Tap them back in the same order (or press 1 to 4). A wrong tap is a miss, and the cell that was due gets a white ring | 1 for each order |
| Colour clash | Pick the colour of the ink, not the word | 1 for every 2 |
| Asteroid tap | Tap or drag across the asteroids (or press Space) | 1 for every 4 |
| Fullest tank | Pick the fullest tank | 1 for every 2 |
| Flight path | Which way does the middle arrow point? | 1 for every 3 |
| Element check | Name the element from its symbol | 1 each |
| Planet order | Going out from the Sun, pick the planet that fills the gap ("Mars → ?"). A row shows the Sun and a slot for each planet, with one planet pictured and the gap glowing | 1 for every 2 |

Every **5 right in a row** earns a bonus credit. If more than 1 in 10 of your answers are wrong, fuel usually gets more expensive. Colour clash has a "Swap round" button for anyone who can't tell colours apart. It shows until your first answer (skipping doesn't count), so skip a card if the colours all look alike.

After the minute comes the **shop**. Buy as many packs as you can pay for, then leave:

| Pack | Price |
|---|---|
| 5 fuel | 10 credits |
| 10 fuel | 18 credits |
| 15 fuel | 25 credits |
| 5 power | 6 credits |

Each fuel stop can only be visited once.

## Possible homes (planets marked !)

1. **Pick a topic:** General knowledge, Mental ability or Technology.
2. **Answer one question** within 60 seconds. If you don't know it, **Skip** swaps it for another of the same topic; the 60 seconds keep running. Outer-ring systems ask easy questions, the middle ring medium ones, and the inner ring hard ones.
3. **Try to land.** The landing chance shows as a bar:
   - Most of it comes from the **time flown**. It starts at 0% and climbs slowly at first, then faster, to 60% at 15 minutes.
   - A **right answer** raises the chance by a tenth of itself (15% becomes 17%, 30% becomes 33%, up to 65%) and pays 15 credits. Early on, when the chance is small, it adds little.
   - A **wrong answer** (or running out of time) halves the chance, and usually makes fuel more expensive.

   | Time flown | Chance from time |
   |---|---|
   | 0:00 | 0% |
   | 5:00 | 7% |
   | 7:30 | 15% |
   | 10:00 | 27% |
   | 12:30 | 42% |
   | 15:00 | 60% |

4. **Choose how to land:**
   - **Land blind** (free): one landing site, one chance.
   - **Scan first** (1 power): if the first site is toxic, the scanner tries a second one.
5. **The landing.** A 2.6 second film plays: the shuttle drops from orbit to the ground. Then the readings come in one by one, while a scan ring pulses across the ground. If the toxic elements are within the safe limit, the world is **habitable**: the sky turns green and you win. If not, the sky turns to red smog, the failed landing costs **30 seconds** (the film's own time is given back), and you can't try that planet again.

## Strategy

- **Early landings are long shots.** The chance starts at 0%, so the first minutes are for scouting, refuelling and getting into position.
- **But time is points.** Every 10 seconds left is a point, so waiting for better odds costs score. That's the game's main trade-off.
- **Outer systems ask easier questions** (more right answers, so a tenth more instead of halving), but their lanes cost the most fuel.
- **Hop when planets line up.** The same hop can cost much less a few seconds later.
- **Keep enough fuel to leave.** Being stranded ends the run.
- **Wrong answers cost twice:** they halve the landing chance and usually raise fuel prices.

## The display board

Finished runs appear on the display board (`/board`) within a few seconds. Every win ranks above every loss. Wins rank by points. Losses rank by more worlds visited (fuel stops count), then more fuel left, then who lasted longer.

## Controls and accessibility

- Questions and choice cards: tap, or press **A to D** or **1 to 4**.
- Reactor tap: keys **1 to 4** tap the cells, left to right and top to bottom; each cell shows its number. Asteroid tap: **Space** hits the leading asteroid.
- **Escape** skips the intro film and the How to play reel.
- First-time tips explain each part of the game with a short clip, and the clock waits while a tip is open.
- With reduced motion turned on, the intro film is skipped, the How to play reel shows still frames, and Asteroid tap is left out of the fuel games. The landing film, the live planets, comets, shooting stars and the low-air pulse are left out too, and the loss pictures hold still. Phones keep the still planet pictures.
- The **Sound** button (bottom right) turns sound on and off, and the game remembers the choice.
